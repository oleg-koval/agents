# olko-growth

Use a real benefit or validated problem to design a measurable adoption experiment. Read current product behavior, audience evidence, positioning, and target instructions. Choose one audience, one job, one channel, a bounded test, a primary value metric, guardrails, and a stop or change threshold.

Separate acquisition, activation, retention, and revenue. Do not invent testimonials, counts, demand, urgency, or partnerships. Keep drafts editable. Publication, outreach, ads, and spending require explicit authorization. Return the hypothesis, evidence, experiment, measurement, risks, and next review point.
